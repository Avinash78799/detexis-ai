    Delete account and monitoring data
    This permanently removes your account, all websites in this workspace, sensor keys, request history, incidents, review notes, scan results and sensor-health history from the live database. All devices are signed out.
    Remove the sensor from your applications first. Deletion does not change your website. Existing backup copies follow the operator’s retention policy; previously delivered external alerts cannot be recalled.
    This action cannot be undone. Save any history you need before continuing.
    Save your replacement keys
    These private keys are shown once. Save them in your server’s secret settings before leaving or reloading this page. Refreshing the website list keeps them here.
    Save your sensor credentials
    Use each site's credentials in that application's secret settings. They stay here while you refresh the website list. Save them before leaving or reloading the page.

    ShieldMiddleware,
    api_url=os.environ["SHIELD_URL"],
    api_key=os.environ["SHIELD_API_KEY"],
    site_salt=os.environ["SHIELD_SALT"],
    outage_retry_window=30,
